# GLIDE Whitepaper

## Vision

Reaching traditional equities from a crypto wallet is still scattered and fiddly. GLIDE imagines one coin that acts as the single way in: hold it, and a curated slate of equities, capped at ten, becomes reachable through one connected ecosystem.

## Mission

Keep the path short, the slate small, and the terms plain. Details are published only once they are confirmed.
