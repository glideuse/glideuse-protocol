 How it works 
 
 
 Launch You pick the anchor, up to ten payout stocks, a fee of 1, 2 or 2.5% and a first buy, then sign
 one payment. The coin launches through the Float program on Raydium LaunchLab, and your first buy lands in your wallet. 
 Trade Every buy and sell pays the fee. Float splits it the same way for every coin: 15% to the holders in
 the anchor, 5% creator share, 35% to $FLOAT burns, 35% to traders and 10% to the treasury. 
 Payout The 5% creator share lands in a wallet that belongs to this coin alone, and goes where you chose: to
 the holders in the stocks you picked, to you, to any wallet, to a charity, or a mix. Nobody claims anything.
